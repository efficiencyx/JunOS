Who is responsible

Jun OS is an unofficial fan project. Two different parties are involved, and it matters which is which:

You

Jun OS is intended as a private installation. You control its machine, database and accounts. Your account, locally stored conversations, memory and settings are not available to the maintainer unless you expose them yourself or explicitly consent to the separate sharing described below.

The maintainer

Andrea Torelli, of andrealab.it, Italy, operates the metrics server. He is the data controller only for copies you explicitly share with that server. Cloudflare processes network traffic on his behalf as an infrastructure processor for content and customer logs, and describes certain network data it creates as data for which it acts as controller.

Privacy contact andrea@andrealab.it Withdrawal, erasure, access — or just a question.

What sharing sends

Only if you explicitly turn on Share my chats for training. Then, for each future message you send:

↑ Sent

  • Up to the first 8,192 bytes of what you typed and the model's raw reply, including sexual or otherwise intimate content and any information about real people that you put in the chat
  • The raw reply is captured before hidden control tags are removed from the visible message. It can therefore contain action tags, relationship bookkeeping and memory text that the model proposes saving from the conversation
  • A 👍 or 👎 if you rate a reply
  • Jun's three relationship gauges — affection, trust, tension — as they stood before and after the message, since they are part of what the model is told and a shared turn means nothing without them
  • Which model and provider answered, the context size, token counts, timings, reasoning setting, request route and idle-state flag
  • A random installation id; a per-account tag derived from it; conversation, turn and time tags, so turns and later ratings can be associated correctly
  • Your connection IP address and request metadata are necessarily processed by Cloudflare and the origin server. The Jun payload has no IP field; the origin retains only a daily-salted IP hash for abuse protection

✕ Not read from your account

  • Your account email address, password or name as separate fields — but anything you type into a shared chat is included in its text
  • The name you gave Jun as a separate field
  • Your existing saved memory notes, journal and wardrobe as separate fields. A proposed memory written inside the raw model reply is included as described above
  • The text of conversations from before you turned sharing on

Turning sharing on is never retroactive. Old conversation text stays where it is. If you later rate an older reply while sharing is on, only the rating and its turn tag may be sent; the older text is not uploaded.

Is it anonymous?

No — and we would rather say so than reassure you incorrectly.

The data is pseudonymous: it carries no directly identifying field, but the installation id is stable, so everything shared from one install stays linked together, and free-typed chat text can itself contain personal details you chose to write. Treat shared chats as personal data that happens not to be labelled with your name — which is exactly why we ask before taking any, and why you can have it deleted.

Why, and on what basis

The purposes are to prepare and review a training corpus, evaluate Jun's responses, train Jun LoRA adapters and, after the assessment described below, potentially publish those adapters. Shared chats are not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to profile you.

The legal basis is your consent: art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, and art. 9(2)(a) for the parts that reveal your sex life or sexual orientation, which intimate roleplay generally does. Providing this data is voluntary, is not required by a contract or by law, and saying no has no effect on any Jun feature. Consent is the only basis relied on for your submitted data, so you withdraw it rather than objecting to consent-based processing.

Information about other people

Your consent covers personal data about you. It cannot give consent for somebody else. Do not put identifiable information about another real person in a chat you share, especially information about their health, sex life, sexual orientation, beliefs or other private circumstances. Use fictional details instead.

If a shared chat nevertheless contains another person's data, its source is the Jun user who typed it. That data is not intentionally sought for training. If you believe the corpus contains information about you that somebody else supplied, email the privacy contact so it can be located, restricted and, where required, erased. This section also makes the information required by art. 14 GDPR publicly available to affected non-users.

Your choices and rights

Withdraw consent Settings → Privacy
As easy to take back as it was to give. Sharing stops immediately, and declining does not disable any feature of Jun.
Erasure art. 17 Settings → Privacy → Request deletion
Turns sharing off locally and attempts to send the metrics server a request to delete telemetry retained for your account. The in-app “requested” status confirms the request was initiated, not that deletion has finished. Email the privacy contact if you want confirmation or the request appears not to complete.
Access, rectification, restriction, portability art. 15, 16, 18, 20
Email the contact above. Account-specific requests require both the installation id from TELEMETRY_INSTALL_ID in your .env and the user_ref derived from that id and your local numeric account id. The in-app erasure request supplies both automatically; email the contact for help if you cannot retrieve them.
Complain art. 77
To your national data protection authority, if you think this notice or the handling of your data is wrong — in Italy, where the maintainer is based, that is the Garante per la Protezione dei Dati Personali.

How long it is kept

Shared chat turns 36 months
Then deleted automatically, whether or not you ask. Ratings are kept alongside the turn they refer to and go with it. You can have yours deleted sooner at any time.
IP address hashes 30 days
Your IP address is not stored in plain form. The server receives it as part of the network connection and converts it into a short-lived daily-salted hash, used solely for abuse protection and cleared after 30 days.
Backups 30 days
Backups containing shared turns expire within 30 days. They are isolated from ordinary use. If a backup is restored after an erasure, recorded deletions are applied again before the corpus returns to use.

These periods are enforced by scheduled pruning. The live database and its backups are stored in Italy on an encrypted hypervisor storage layer. Encryption protects powered-off storage and backup copies; authorised processing still occurs while the server is running.

Where the data goes

The JSON payload goes through Cloudflare to the metrics server named in TELEMETRY_ENDPOINT. The origin server and all corpus backups are operated by the maintainer in Italy. Cloudflare processes connection IP addresses, request metadata and content in transit as an infrastructure processor and may process data in the United States or other countries through its global network and subprocessors.

For EEA-to-US transfers, Cloudflare states that it relies on its certification under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and, if that certification is unavailable, the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses with supplementary measures. Its current arrangements are described in Cloudflare's Data Processing Addendum and privacy policy.

Shared chats are not disclosed to advertising or third-party analytics services. Cloudflare and its subprocessors process content and customer logs to deliver and protect the metrics endpoint. Cloudflare describes its separate processing of network data, and its role for that data, in the privacy policy linked above.

Sharing is transmitted over HTTPS, and reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect what is stored — though no transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

If you run your own install of Jun OS and point it at a different endpoint, that operator's arrangements apply instead of these — see Other services.

Public LoRA adapters

A LoRA adapter trained on shared chats is not assumed to be anonymous merely because it is smaller than a full model. Before publication, the maintainer documents an assessment of memorisation, extraction and identification risk. An adapter is published only where it is very unlikely that a person can be identified from it, directly or indirectly, and very unlikely that personal training data can be extracted through prompts. An adapter that fails is scrapped or retrained and tested again.

Only an adapter that passes that assessment is treated as anonymous. Official downloads remain available until six months after a tested successor supersedes it. Independent copies made while it was public may remain available afterwards and cannot reliably be recalled. The assessment and the adapter's training lineage are retained so the decision can be reviewed if new evidence of leakage appears.

Other services this app can talk to

Independently of sharing, whichever AI provider you pick is where your messages go. With the bundled Ollama or llama.cpp that is your own machine, and nothing leaves it. If you configure a hosted provider instead — OpenRouter is the one shipped today — your prompts are sent to that provider. As the operator of a private install, you choose that separate processing and should review the provider's role, retention, recipients, international transfers and privacy terms before enabling it. That provider processing is separate from Andrea's metrics collector. Voice and karaoke run locally in sidecar containers.

Automated decisions & children

Jun's replies are generated by a language model, but nothing here makes automated decisions that have legal effects for you, or that similarly significantly affect you, within the meaning of art. 22.

This app displays adult content and is for people aged 18 or over. It is not intended for children, and telemetry from a person known to be under 18 is deleted. The current age gate is a self-declaration, not independent age verification or age assurance.

Changes

If what is collected, why it is used, who receives it or the model-release practice changes materially, the version at the top of this notice is bumped and you are asked to decide again. A previous yes does not carry over.
