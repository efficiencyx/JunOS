# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Jun OS is an adult fan project, and participation in its project spaces is intended for adults (18+) only. We want collaboration to stay welcoming, technically useful, and respectful.

Positive behavior includes:

- treating people with patience and respect;
- giving specific, constructive feedback about the work;
- respecting different experience levels, identities, backgrounds, and viewpoints;
- accepting correction and taking responsibility when something goes wrong;
- protecting other people's privacy and the original creators' intellectual property.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, stalking, discrimination, or personal attacks;
- sexual attention directed at another contributor, or explicit material unrelated to a necessary technical report;
- publishing private conversations, personal information, credentials, or identifying logs without permission;
- distributing recovered game assets or encouraging others to violate the terms in `LICENSE`;
- spam, deliberate disruption, or sustained off-topic arguments.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces where someone is representing the project. Technical disagreement is welcome; hostility toward the people involved is not.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct problems privately to `andrea@andrealab.it`. Include links or screenshots and enough context to understand what happened. Do not open a public issue about another person's conduct.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants whose behavior is harmful. Reports will be reviewed as privately and promptly as this small volunteer project can manage. Retaliation against someone who makes a good-faith report is itself a violation.
